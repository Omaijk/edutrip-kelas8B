Chisingtan Scenic Area, located in Taiwan's Hualien County, is a breathtaking coastal spot known for its crescent-shaped pebble beach and crystal-clear waters. With the backdrop of lush mountains and the sparkling Pacific Ocean, it's a haven for nature lovers seeking tranquility, picturesque views, and outdoor activities like biking along the scenic trails.

Night Market, in the heart of Keelung City, is a bustling hub of Taiwanese street food and lively energy. Famous for its variety of culinary delights such as fresh seafood, bubble tea, and oyster omelets, it's a food lover's paradise. The market's vibrant atmosphere and delicious offerings make it a must-visit for anyone exploring Taiwan's dynamic food culture.
